Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2025-10-26
  Major Features:
    - First stable release!
    - Transparent entities are now clickable/selectable unless transparency is set to 100%
    - At 100% transparency (completely invisible), entities become non-selectable for easy click-through
    - Pipette tool (Q) now gives the normal non-transparent entity when used on transparent variants
  Changes:
    - Rails and supports check their respective transparency settings independently for selectability
    - Improved transparency application to ensure all graphics components are affected
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed rail sprites on rail ramps not being fully transparent at 100% transparency
    - Fixed elevated rail handles and body now properly respond to transparency settings
  Info:
    - Added proper entity localization for all transparent variants
    - Ready for Factorio Mod Portal release
